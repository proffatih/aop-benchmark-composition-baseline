## For the manuscript (source-disjoint, default cut)
  prevalence 50%  ->  PPV 0.74  (74 true actives per 100 positive calls)
  prevalence 10%  ->  PPV 0.24  (24 true actives per 100 positive calls)
  prevalence 5%  ->  PPV 0.13  (13 true actives per 100 positive calls)

  Sensitivity and specificity are properties of the classifier; PPV is not.
  The same model that looks usable on a balanced benchmark is a weak filter
  in a pool where actives are rare -- and this calculation still assumes the
  classifier's sensitivity and specificity transfer unchanged to that pool,
  which our source-disjoint result suggests is itself optimistic.
